Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Zumik mobile app ("Zumik", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Zumik is a student project developed at 42; it is free, shows no ads and sells no data.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, username, display name and, if you set one, a password (stored only as a hash).
  • Data from Google or Facebook sign-in: when you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your account identifier, email address, name and profile picture. We request no other permission and never access your contacts, files or posts.
  • Profile data you choose to add: profile picture, bio, favourite artist and music genres, with per-field visibility settings.
  • Social and music data: the people you follow, your playlists, the tracks you add, your play queue and the music control permissions you grant.
  • Device data: the platform, device model, app version and the device name you choose, used to list your devices and to let you delegate music control.
  • Security data: your IP address and failed sign-in attempts, kept temporarily to rate-limit requests and protect accounts against brute-force attacks.

How we use it

Only to provide the app's features: create and secure your account, sign you in, sync playlists and playback across users and devices, show your profile according to your visibility settings, and send transactional emails (account verification, password reset). We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we never sell it.

Google user data

Zumik uses Google Sign-In only to authenticate you, with the openid, email and profile scopes. The data received is used solely to create and sign in to your Zumik account, is not shared with third parties and is not used to train any AI model. Zumik's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase: database, authentication and profile picture storage.
  • Google and Facebook: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • SoundCloud: music catalogue and streaming; no personal data is sent to it.
  • Gmail: delivery of transactional emails.

No other party receives your personal data.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. Security counters (IP addresses, failed attempts) expire automatically within 24 hours; server logs, which may contain your IP address, are kept only for troubleshooting and security. To delete your account and all associated data, email leon.perrotin@gmail.com from the address linked to your account; it is done within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing. Contact us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to the database is restricted to the Zumik backend.

Children

Zumik is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes, with a new "Last updated" date.
