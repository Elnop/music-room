Zumik

Zumik is a collaborative music app for Android, iOS and the web. Build playlists together in real time and share control of the music playing on your devices with your friends.

Features

  • Playlist Editor: edit a playlist with other people, every change shows up live for everyone.
  • Music Control Delegation: let a friend control the music playing on your device.
  • Friends and profiles: follow friends and choose what each of them can see on your profile.
  • Sign-in: with an email address, a Google account or a Facebook account.

Download

The Android app is available from the latest release.

No install needed on a computer: Open in the browser.
